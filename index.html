Justice for Entrepreneurs
Community Interest Company

We support entrepreneurs and business owners connected to the United Kingdom who face unlawful institutional persecution, abuse of process, or miscarriage of justice — through research and documentation, guidance on legal rights, and the coordination of legal support.

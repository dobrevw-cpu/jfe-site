What we do

We work with the mechanisms that actually deliver — not the ones that merely look impressive.

1 · Documentation & register

Every case enters our register under one rule: fact → document → date. Every claim points to a document. We publish what is verifiable, not rumours.

2 · Signposting

ECtHR, European Commission, sanctions submissions (UK, US, EU), UK procedures — we explain which route fits each case and how to start.

3 · Legal coordination

We connect victims with suitable lawyers and pro bono organisations. We are not a law firm — we are the front door and the coordinator.

4 · Collective submissions

One voice is easy to ignore. An organisation filing dozens of documented cases showing a pattern is not.

5 · Public awareness

Events, publications, media work. Institutional abuse is a pattern, not "isolated incidents" — and the public should know.

6 · Peer support

A network of people who have been through the same — because no one should go through it alone.

Important: JFE CIC is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation or legal advice. We document, signpost, and file collective submissions as an organisation.

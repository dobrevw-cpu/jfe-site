WE ARE LOOKING FOR PEOPLE

This is not a campaign. It is work.

Reading documents, ordering facts, writing to institutions. That work needs people. We are not looking for sympathisers to like posts. We are looking for people who will do something specific.

Who we need

Each of these is a separate job. Read yours and write to us saying what you can do.

Lawyers

Cases arrive told in everyday language. The same case, translated into procedural language, gets read by an institution. Told as a complaint, it gets thrown away.

We need practice in criminal and administrative law: to look at an incoming case and say what in it is legally sound and what is not.

We are not asking you to represent anyone. The company gives no legal advice and is not a law firm.

Economists and accountants

Many cases begin with a tax audit or a withdrawn licence. Behind them sit numbers — turnover, cost, an assessed liability.

We need people who can read an audit report and say where the methodology does not hold. That is the difference between "I say this is unfair" and "here is where the arithmetic fails".

Data and AI specialists

The company holds other people's files. That is not a technical task but a responsibility — under UK law, data about people facing proceedings sits in the heaviest category outside special data.

We need people who can build case intake so that it is safe from day one: where documents live, who has access, what is read automatically and what must not be.

People who can open a door

The company is British. It works with institutions, inspectorates, professional chambers, embassies and international organisations.

We need people with access and experience — in the United Kingdom, in Europe and above all in the United States, where a comparable sanctions mechanism exists.

Journalists and publishers

We publish documents and dated facts, never conclusions about people. It is a slow way of working, but a verifiable one.

We need people who know how a document becomes readable text without ceasing to be accurate.

People who will simply do the work

Translation. Laying out documents. Checking dates. Tracking whether an answer ever came.

None of it requires a degree. All of it is needed.

How you join

You do not have to decide everything at once. You start at the lightest level.

Member and directorA full vote and a part in running the company.Responsibility under UK law. By assessment, after a conversation.
Membership means directorship. Under the articles, a "member" of the company is a director — with a full vote and personal responsibility under UK company law. It is not a formality and is not granted automatically. If you want to take part at that level, write and we will discuss it in person.

Financial support

We are looking for that too. Money is a contribution exactly as time, knowledge and contacts are — simply in another form.

The company is still being built. For now there is no account for donations. If you would like to support the work financially, write to info@jfe.org.uk and we will arrange it. A bank account or PayPal will appear here shortly.

The legal form is not an accident. The company's assets carry an asset lock — they cannot pass to any private person, the founder included. There are no shares, no dividend and no stake to sell. Any surplus returns to the work, and a report goes to the CIC Regulator every year. We do not ask you to take our integrity on trust — it is a matter of record, and anyone can check it at Companies House under number 17372978.

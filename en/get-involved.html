Get involved

Four ways to help — from one minute of your time to full membership.

Follow and share

The fastest help: follow our Facebook page and share the cases. Visibility is protection.

Become a member

Membership is by a nominal £1 guarantee — your entire financial liability. Members vote and control the organisation. Write to us for details.

Offer expertise

Lawyers, accountants, journalists, AI and technology specialists — every skill helps. Tell us what you can do.

Support the work

The organisation operates on a non-profit basis — all support goes to the cause and is publicly accounted for.
